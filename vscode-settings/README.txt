Copy settings.json into the project's .vscode folder:

    mkdir -p ~/Downloads/CSE299_Project/.vscode
    cp ~/Downloads/CSE299_Project/vscode-settings/settings.json \
       ~/Downloads/CSE299_Project/.vscode/

Optional. It just sets a bigger font, hides the minimap, turns on sticky
scroll (keeps the function name pinned at the top while you scroll), and
points the Python interpreter at your myproject venv.
